A FIELD GUIDE

Your hands. Your world.

01 / MOVE

Guide the little light.

Use A / D or arrow keys, and Space to jump. With hand controls on, move left or right of your calibrated center. Pinch thumb and index finger to jump.

02 / BORROW

Make the ordinary useful.

With Auto weave on, hold an object inside the camera guide near a gap. Gemini checks snapshots and a suitable object builds the bridge immediately. Books equip shields, shiny items reflect, and phones or pens equip strikes automatically.

03 / WEAVE

Close the distance.

Walk to a glowing gap anchor and hold a phone, notebook, ruler or cardboard in view. Your hero waits at the edge while Auto weave checks. When the bridge appears, keep moving. E and Weave bridge remain available for manually borrowed powers.

04 / FACE THE WARDEN

Defense becomes attack.

The Warden appears in first person over your camera. Hold a recognized book or broad object to bounce attacks back. Sweep a recognized phone or pen across the camera to strike. Local prop motion works even when fingers are hidden. Keep the camera stationary. A book and pen can be recognized together. Pinch or Space dodges. Show a new item to switch powers automatically.

Keep held items inside the camera guide. Fingers need to be visible only for hand steering and pinching. Calibration sets your neutral point. Losing tracking stops gesture input. Esc pauses or closes a dialog. Sound is optional. Practice items never pretend to be live AI results.
