# Two-minute live demo

Have a book, phone and pen ready. Connect the camera and leave Live items enabled. Wait for the equipped indicators before demonstrating each power. Bracketed directions are actions, not spoken lines.

[Hold up the book, then the pen.]

"This book is my shield. This pen is my sword. And the objects on my desk are my inventory.

This is Riftmender: a camera-powered adventure where you borrow abilities from the real world."

[Approach the first gap.]

"The path is broken. My character needs a way across. Instead of finding an item inside the game, I can bring something from outside it."

[Hold the phone inside the guide. Wait for the bridge.]

"Gemini evaluates the object's visible properties. A flat surface can become a bridge. Once it qualifies, the path builds automatically. Show the object, borrow its power, keep playing."

[Cross the bridge. Use Boss demo to jump ahead if necessary.]

"Then the relationship changes. The world comes to us."

[Raise the book. Wait for SHIELD and an incoming attack.]

"The Rift Warden appears over the camera feed. This book becomes a shield. The blast reaches it and returns to the boss. Defense becomes an attack."

[Show the pen alongside the book. Wait for STRIKE, then sweep it smoothly.]

"The pen becomes a blade. I can combine a defensive object and an attacking object, then strike when the core opens."

[Keep playing while explaining.]

"Underneath, we separate understanding from movement. Gemini's Decision API evaluates which abilities the objects qualify for. A separate vision call locates them. Local camera tracking handles movement between those checks, and the game applies the rules."

[Finish on a reflection, a strike or victory.]

"Riftmender turns everyday objects into meaningful game mechanics. Your world is the inventory."

## If recognition hesitates

Keep the item inside the guide and say: "The model checks snapshots periodically. I'm waiting for the equipped indicator before using this item."

If live recognition cannot continue, explicitly switch to **Practice without Gemini** and say: "I'll show the remaining game mechanics with manually equipped practice items." Use K to guard and J to strike. Do not describe practice results as live recognition.
